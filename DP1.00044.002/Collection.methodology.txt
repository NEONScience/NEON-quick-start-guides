Precipitation - weighing gauge v2 measurements are housed within a small double fence intercomparison reference, which is generally located within 0.5 km of the tower infrastructure. The weighing gauge collector uses a load cell to continuously measure the weight of accumulated precipitation in the collector bucket, which is converted to a precipitation depth based on the surface area of the collector. Bulk precipitation is determined at one-minute and thirty-minute intervals for precipitation - weighing gauge v2. The Documentation section of this guide includes a list of precipitation collector types and quantities at each NEON site, along with the nearest terrestrial site for accessing precipitation data for aquatic sites without collectors (NEON Precipitation Sensors by Site).
