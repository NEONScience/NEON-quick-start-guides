NEON's backbone taxonomy: versioned lists of the taxa that NEON field and laboratory staff may record for each of eleven taxon types (algae, beetles, birds, fish, herpetofauna, macroinvertebrates, mosquitoes, mosquito pathogens, plants, small mammals, and ticks), with accepted names, synonymy, taxonomic hierarchy, naming authority, and the nativity status of each taxon at each NEON site. This is a reference taxonomy, not a checklist of observed taxa: a taxon's presence in a site's backbone means it is within scope for identification at that site, not that it has been observed there.
