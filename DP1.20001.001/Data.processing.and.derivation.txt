Once field data are reviewed, verified, and linked (Habitat Survey and Pebble Count Survey application records), spatial data are post-processed prior to data publication. Pathfinder software is used to derive post-processed coordinates through differential correction, a process that compares field-collected GPS observations with data from a stationary reference base station at a known location. This correction reduces position error caused by satellite, atmospheric, and timing effects, resulting in improved coordinate accuracy. For each survey, the nearest available Continuously Operating Reference Station (CORS) within NOAA's National CORS Network (NCN) is used as the reference base station. The corrected coordinates are reported in both latitude/longitude and UTM formats and are provided along with associated correction metadata and positional uncertainty values.
