"Non-NA values in the pebbleCountD5, pebbleCountD16, pebbleCountD50, and pebbleCountD84 fields in the rap_pebbleFieldData table indicate that the expected number of 200 samples were collected during the Pebble Count Survey. The maxPDOP and maxHDOP values in the rap_rapidHabitatAssessment table reflect a mathematical representation of satellite geometry during data collection and can be used to assess the accuracy of the GPS signal (accuracy increases with lower values). The surveyPointElevationUncertainty and surveyPointCoordinateUncertainty values in the rap_rapidHabitatAssessment table represent the uncertainty surrounding the elevation and coordinates calculated for each waypoint collected during the Habitat Survey (accuracy increases with lower values). The gnssStDev value in the rap_rapidHabitatAssessment table represents the spread of Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) positions averaged for each waypoint. This standard deviation contributes to the surveyPointCoordinateUncertainty, with lower values representing more accurate coordinates and higher data quality.
