Rapid habitat assessment data can be used to quantify physical change in streams across temporal and spatial scales. The location and frequency of different aquatic habitat types can be obtained and defining characteristics (e.g. canopy cover, dominant substrata, max pool depth, etc.) can be quantified using data in the rap_rapidHabitatAssessment table. Additionally, distribution curves can be constructed to assess changes in particle size distribution over time using data from the rap_pebbleFieldData table.
